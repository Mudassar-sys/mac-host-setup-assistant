      Why this wins, in plain terms

      We read your post line by line, then checked it against how macOS and BlueBubbles actually behave. Here is where the post and reality differ, and what we do about it.

      You asked

      Send the plain text BlueBubbles password back to the backend.For a firm that guards wealthy clients, one leak of that store ends trust.

      We do better

      The app generates a strong password, saves it in the Mac Keychain, and sends it encrypted.The user never types it and never sees it. Your n8n still gets what it needs.

      You asked

      Configure or guide the webhook insertion.The word guide suggests you expect manual copy paste.

      We do better

      BlueBubbles has a local REST API. We register both webhooks through it, fully automatic.Zero copy paste, zero wrong URLs.

      You asked

      Boot into Recovery and disable SIP as part of setup.Framed as a standard step for everyone.

      We do better

      SIP is only for Private API extras like reactions and typing. Plain send, receive and webhooks work with SIP on.Most users skip Recovery Mode entirely.

      Not in the post

      Full Disk Access cannot be granted silently.Apple TCC blocks it. Any bid that promises to automate it will fail on day one.

      We do better

      The app opens the exact settings panel and waits, then verifies the grant before moving on.Honest about the one thing macOS reserves for the user.

      Not in the post

      Your app itself will be blocked by Gatekeeper.Without signing and notarization macOS calls it damaged.

      We do better

      We ship it signed and notarized so it opens clean.We confirm your Apple Developer account before build.

      Not in the post

      You cannot see who is stuck where.Silent drop off is the real reason onboarding fails.

      The Operator View shows every host and its exact step, live from your Supabase.You rescue users before they quit.
